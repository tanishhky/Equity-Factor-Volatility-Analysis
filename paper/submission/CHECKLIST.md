# Launch checklist

## 1. SSRN revision (optional, recommended first; free)
SSRN > My Papers > 7113079 > Revise. Paste the fields in `SSRN_revision.md`, upload `paper/letter.pdf`. Elsevier allows preprints, so posting before submission is fine.

## 2. Finance Research Letters (Editorial Manager; USD 200 submission fee)
Single-anonymized review: the manuscript keeps the author's name. Upload:
- Manuscript: `paper/letter.pdf` (and `letter.tex` with `generated/` and `images/` if a source file is requested)
- Highlights: `highlights.txt` (5 bullets, each at most 85 characters)
- Cover letter: `cover_letter.pdf`
- Declarations (form fields): `declarations.md`
- Keywords: volatility-managed portfolios; momentum; time-varying beta; Tweedie's formula; factor timing. JEL: G11, G12, G14.
Limits checked: abstract 179 words (limit 250); main text about 1,600 words (limit 2,500, excluding abstract, tables, figures, captions, references and declarations).

## 3. After SSRN shows the revision
Update the site's research page and post on LinkedIn (drafted on request).
