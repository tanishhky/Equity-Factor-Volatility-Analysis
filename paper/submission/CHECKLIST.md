# Launch checklist

## 1. SSRN revision of 7113079 (free; do this first)
ssrn.com > sign in > My Papers > 7113079 > Revise. Paste the fields in `SSRN_revision.md`, upload `paper/letter.pdf`, confirm. Elsevier allows preprints, so posting before submission is fine.

## 2. Finance Research Letters (USD 200 submission fee)
1. Open https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/finance-research-letters and click **Submit your article**. Use that button, not an old bookmark: editorialmanager.com/frl showed "Site under development. Do not use for live manuscript submission" on 2026-09-28.
2. Sign in or register with the Elsevier account for tanishkyadav@nyu.edu.
3. Start a new submission; choose the research article type; enter title, abstract (from the manuscript), keywords (volatility-managed portfolios; momentum; time-varying beta; Tweedie's formula; factor timing) and JEL codes G11, G12, G14.
4. Upload:
   - Manuscript: `paper/letter.pdf`; if an editable source is requested, `manuscript_source.zip` (compiles on its own)
   - Highlights: `highlights.txt` (5 bullets, each at most 85 characters)
   - Cover letter: `cover_letter.pdf`
   - Declarations (competing interest, funding, CRediT, data availability): fill from `declarations.md`; the AI declaration is already in the manuscript
5. Review is single-anonymized, so the manuscript keeps the author's name.
6. Pay the submission fee, check the system-built PDF, approve and submit.

Limits checked: abstract 179 words (limit 250); main text about 1,700 words (limit 2,500, excluding abstract, tables, figures, captions, references and declarations); 3 tables, 3 figures, 8 pages.

Update the site's research page and post on LinkedIn (drafted on request).
